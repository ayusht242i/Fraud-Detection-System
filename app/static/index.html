Fraud Watch

An AI assistant that scores every card payment and sends the suspicious ones to a person for review. It never blocks a payment on its own.

1. Score
Every payment gets a risk score from 0 to 100, based on patterns learned from past fraud.
2. Flag
Payments at or above the alert line are sent to a person. Nothing is blocked automatically.
3. Review
A person checks each flagged payment. Every flag is also recorded for an audit trail.
